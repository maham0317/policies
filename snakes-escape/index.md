# Privacy Policy for Snakes Escape

**Effective date:** 19 August 2026
**Hosted at:** https://maham0317.github.io/policies/snakes-escape/
**Developer:** GulzarDev
**Contact:** gulzardev40@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Snakes Escape** (“the App”, “we”,
“us”) handles information. We have built the App to need as little of your data as
possible. Please read this policy before using the App.

## 1. Information the App itself collects
**None that personally identifies you.** Snakes Escape does not ask you to create an
account, log in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address,
phone number, or location.

The only information the App stores is your **game progress and settings** (your current
level and your music/sound preferences). This is saved **locally on your device only**
(using the device’s standard app‑preferences storage). It never leaves your device, and
we cannot see it. Uninstalling the App removes it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App displays ads provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob
SDK and its partners may collect and process certain information automatically, which can
include:

- a device **advertising identifier** (e.g. Google Advertising ID),
- general device information (device model, operating‑system version, language,
  coarse/approximate region derived from IP),
- ad‑interaction and diagnostics data (which ads were shown, clicked, and basic
  performance/crash signals).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is used to deliver ads, limit how often
you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and reduce fraud. We do not receive or store
this information ourselves beyond aggregate, non‑identifying reports.

Google’s handling of this data is governed by **Google’s Privacy Policy**:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google’s advertising information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings:
**Settings → Google → Ads** (reset advertising ID / opt out of ad personalization).

## 3. Consent for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland
If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
App shows you a Google‑certified consent form **before any ad is requested**, using
Google’s **User Messaging Platform (UMP)**. Your choice controls whether Google may use
your advertising identifier and similar data to show you **personalised** ads.

- If you **consent**, ads may be personalised using the data described in Section 2.
- If you **decline**, ads are still shown but are **non‑personalised** — Google then uses
  only the minimum data needed to deliver an ad, cap how often you see it, and prevent
  fraud. Declining never limits your access to any part of the game.

**Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6).** Personalised advertising relies on your **consent**
(Art. 6(1)(a)). Basic ad delivery, frequency capping, security and fraud prevention rely
on **legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)). Your local game progress is stored on your own
device and is not processed by us at all.

**Who is responsible.** For advertising data, **Google** acts as the data controller — see
Google’s Privacy Policy linked in Section 2. GulzarDev does not receive, store, or have
access to your personal data.

### How to withdraw or change your consent
- **Reset or opt out of your advertising ID** on your device:
  **Settings → Google → Ads** → *Reset advertising ID* or *Opt out of ad personalisation*.
  This applies immediately and to every app on the device.
- **Uninstall the App**, which removes all locally stored game data.

You may also contact us at the address in Section 10 with any question about your consent,
and we will point you to the right control or to Google where the data sits with them.

## 4. In‑app purchases
The App currently contains **no in‑app purchases** and does not process any payment or
financial information. If a paid option (such as an ad‑free upgrade) is added in a future
version, it will be handled by **Google Play Billing**, we will not receive your payment
card or financial details, and this policy will be updated before that version ships.

## 5. We do not sell your data
We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect it ourselves. Any data
processing related to ads is performed by Google as described above.

## 6. Children’s privacy
Snakes Escape is a family‑friendly puzzle game suitable for all ages. It is **not directed
at collecting personal information from children**, and we do not knowingly collect
personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App shows ads served
by Google, we configure ad requests for a general audience. Parents who prefer their child
not receive personalised ads can opt out of ad personalisation in the device settings
described in Section 3. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the ad network, please contact us and we will help you take
appropriate steps with Google.

## 7. Data security & retention
Game progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.
Data transmitted by the advertising SDK is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and is
retained and managed by Google according to Google’s own policies.

## 8. Your rights
Because the App stores no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to export
or delete on our side. You can:
- clear all local game data by uninstalling the App, and
- reset or opt out of your advertising identifier in your device settings (Section 2).

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you also have the rights to access, correct,
erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and
to withdraw consent at any time (Section 3). Because advertising data is held by Google as
controller, please direct such requests to Google via its Privacy Policy; we will help
where we can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection
authority.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at the URL above with
a new effective date.
